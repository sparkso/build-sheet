Working w/ the DS Team · his lane

  • I get their science out of the notebook and in front of clients.
  • I know when NOT to use an LLM — validated model? I deploy theirs.
  • I plug into their governance, never around it. No shadow models.
  • Eval is shared: they define “correct,” I build the harness.
